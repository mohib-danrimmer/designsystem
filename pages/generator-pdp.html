About this Generator

Powerol, India's most trusted diesel generator brand from the house of Mahindra, offers unmatched performance and reliability across its silent DG Set range. DAZZLE Power Solutions is an authorised Powerol dealer and service partner in Maharashtra, with over a decade of experience supplying critical power infrastructure to manufacturing plants, commercial complexes, hospitals, and data centres.

The Powerol Silent Series features a heavy-duty acoustic enclosure that brings noise levels below 75 dB(A) at 1 metre — making it suitable for noise-sensitive environments. Equipped with Mahindra's fuel-efficient BSVI-compliant engines and an Auto Mains Failure (AMF) panel, these sets deliver seamless power transfer within seconds of a grid outage.
